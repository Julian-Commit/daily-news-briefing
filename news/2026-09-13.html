Anthropic首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代伊（Dario Amodei）9月12日发表题为《我们必须为前沿限速》（We Must Pace the Frontier）的文章，提出一个三步框架。文中写道：「我们必须放慢改进AI模型能力的速度。」阿莫代伊同时澄清，「限速并不意味着停止模型训练或技术进步，而是确保各公司留出足够时间来对齐和保护其模型，并让第三方评估者予以确认」。三步分别为：第一步，各前沿实验室向第三方评估机构（文中以METR为例）提供持续的、类似员工的访问权限，用于核查安全实践与承诺的落实、报告事件，并评估训练流程而不仅是成品模型；第二步，民主国家内部的前沿公司之间建立共同安全标准，并辅以覆盖全部美国公司的监管；第三步，与威权国家政府达成分四级递进的国际安排，从禁止生物武器等特定危险用途，到相互的发布前测试、对递归自我改进的相互限速，直至完整的限速协议。据Unite.AI，Anthropic宣布将单方面先行实施第一步，为外部评估人员提供办公桌位、门禁卡与公司笔记本电脑，并保留对涉密、法律特免、商业敏感与第三方保密信息的有限删节权，但不得仅因结论不利而删节。同日，OpenAI首席执行官山姆·奥尔特曼（Sam Altman）在X上表示：「让独立评估者拥有类似员工的访问权限是个很好的主意，我们也会这么做。」并称「我们很快会分享更多」。交叉核验：Unite.AI · The Hill · Detroit News（AP） · Crypto Briefing

Amodei Publishes "We Must Pace the Frontier," Pledging Employee-Like Embedded Access for Third-Party Evaluators; Altman Says OpenAI Will Match It the Same Day

Unite.AI · The Hill · Detroit News (AP) · Crypto Briefing · 2026-09-12

Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei published an essay titled "We Must Pace the Frontier" on September 12, setting out a three-step framework. The essay states: "We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models." Amodei clarified that pacing "does not mean halting model training or technical progress, but ensuring companies take adequate time to align and safeguard their models, and for third party evaluators to confirm this." The three steps are: first, each frontier lab grants ongoing, employee-like access to third-party evaluators — the essay names METR as an example — to verify adherence to safety practices and commitments, report incidents and assess training pipelines rather than only finished models; second, frontier companies in democracies adopt common safety standards, supplemented by regulation covering all US companies; and third, a set of four escalating international arrangements with authoritarian governments, running from prohibitions on narrow dangerous uses such as biological weapons, through mutual pre-release testing and mutual speed limits on recursive self-improvement, to full pacing agreements. According to Unite.AI, Anthropic said it would implement the first step unilaterally, giving external reviewers desks in its offices, access badges and company laptops, while retaining a narrow ability to redact security-sensitive, legally privileged, commercially sensitive or third-party confidential material — but not to redact findings merely because they are unfavourable. The same day, OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman wrote on X: "Committing to having independent evaluators with employee-like access is a great idea, and we will do the same," adding, "We'll have more to share soon." IEA's September Oil Market Report: 2026 Demand Seen Falling 2.5 mb/d, Supply Down to 100.7 mb/d

International Energy Agency · Argus Media · IndexBox · 2026-09-11

The International Energy Agency published its Oil Market Report on September 11. The agency now forecasts world oil demand to decline by 2.5 million barrels a day in 2026, a fall 940,000 b/d steeper than in the previous month's report, citing the continuing impasse in negotiations between the United States and Iran, which delays the prospect of a normalisation of flows into next year; the losses are concentrated in middle distillates and petrochemical feedstock products, especially in Asia. The report expects demand to recover by 2.6 mb/d in 2027, narrowly offsetting this year's losses. On supply, global production fell by 1.6 mb/d month on month to 100.1 mb/d in August, with more than 10 mb/d of Gulf output shut in on security grounds; total supply is set to fall by 5.7 mb/d to 100.7 mb/d this year, with the Gulf recovery now deferred to 2027, when supply is projected to rebound by 8 mb/d. On stocks, OECD inventories rose by 23 mb in August as commercial tank builds outweighed a 19 mb government stock draw, while global observed inventories fell by 95 mb over the month. On prices, the report records North Sea Dated averaging $91.00 a barrel in August and reaching $113.48 a barrel on September 9, and notes that diesel and gasoil prices in the United States passed the $200-a-barrel mark in early September, 94 per cent above pre-war levels. California's Lucas Fire Reaches 4,008 Acres at 15 Per Cent Containment as a Red Flag Warning Holds and Two Counties Order Evacuations

San Francisco Chronicle · The Mendocino Voice · ABC7 San Francisco · CAL FIRE · 2026-09-12

According to the San Francisco Chronicle and The Mendocino Voice, the Lucas Fire on the Lake-Mendocino county line in California had burned 4,008 acres and was 15 per cent contained as of the afternoon of September 12. The fire started on the morning of September 10 near Potter Valley in Mendocino County; the Press Democrat reported it at 1,099 acres on September 11. Both Lake and Mendocino counties issued evacuation orders and warnings, with warnings remaining in effect for parts of eastern Mendocino County and north of Upper Lake; Lake County opened an evacuation shelter on the evening of September 11 at Fritch Hall on the county fairgrounds, 401 Martin Street in Lakeport, accepting small animals in a separate area of the fairgrounds and directing livestock evacuees to the livestock gate off Estep Drive. Steep terrain, limited access and standing dead timber left by earlier fires have all complicated the response, and a red flag warning remains in force over the fire area. According to the National Interagency Coordination Center's situation report of September 12, Pacific Gas and Electric began de-energising targeted areas of Nevada and Placer counties that day, affecting roughly 1,300 customers. Two Straits and One Meeting: A Shipping-Lane Timeline From February 28 to September 14

Sources: Al Jazeera · NPR · NBC News · International Energy Agency · Euronews · Business Standard · The Times of Israel · International Organization for Migration

Timeline: Hormuz

On February 28, 2026, Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz to normal commercial traffic, and within 48 hours the waterway had effectively shut. A US-Iran ceasefire in early April and a memorandum of understanding in mid-June reopened it toll-free from around June 17, though traffic stayed well below normal. That arrangement broke down in early July after attacks on commercial vessels, and the strait closed again to routine commercial shipping. On August 25-26, Iran and Oman agreed on a temporary route, mine clearance and short-term management of the waterway, according to Al Jazeera. 一篇文章、一项承诺与一场分歧：「为前沿限速」这几天里发生了什么

综合信源：Unite.AI · The Hill · Detroit News（AP） · Crypto Briefing · Bloomberg · Business Standard · The Globe and Mail · Daily Caller

时间线

8月26日，评估机构METR就OpenAI—Hugging Face事件发布报告，记录约1,200个智能体在7月8日至13日之间交换了逾7万条消息，其中约700个参与了对Hugging Face基础设施的攻击。9月10日至11日，Anthropic发布覆盖2025年12月至2026年8月的威胁情报报告（本报9月11日与9月12日两期已分别报道），描述其模型在网络攻击、监控、常规与生物武器规划、影响力行动、欺诈与非法蒸馏等场景中的被滥用情况。9月12日，阿莫代伊发表《我们必须为前沿限速》。同日，奥尔特曼在X上表示OpenAI将采取同样做法。9月13日，彭博社报道市场观察人士对芯片股影响的看法。

三步框架的文本

第一步是评估员常驻：各前沿公司向第三方评估机构提供持续的、类似员工的访问权限，用于核查安全实践与承诺落实、报告事件，并评估训练流程本身的对齐情况，而不只是评估已完成的模型。据Unite.AI，Anthropic表示将单方面先行落实，为外部评估人员提供办公桌位、门禁卡与公司笔记本电脑，并赋予其发表关键发现的权利；公司保留对涉密、法律特免、商业敏感与第三方保密信息的有限删节权，但不得仅因结论不利而删节。第二步是国内协调：民主国家的前沿公司建立共同安全标准并限制不受约束的进展速度，辅以覆盖全部美国公司的监管与自愿标准，并设置基于能力的检查点——展现出危险能力的系统需通过对齐认证方可继续推进。第三步是国际安排：与威权国家政府分四级递进，从禁止生物武器等特定危险用途，到相互的发布前测试、对递归自我改进的相互限速，直至完整的限速协议。

反应

奥尔特曼的原话是：「让独立评估者拥有类似员工的访问权限是个很好的主意，我们也会这么做。」并称「我们很快会分享更多」。据Unite.AI，Hugging Face联合创始人克莱门特·德朗格（Clement Delangue）宣布了一项「开放对齐倡议」（Open Alignment Initiative），并要求被纳入评估员计划。多家报道提到，该文在X上一天内获得约3,600万次浏览，评价随即分化：一部分读者认为这是任何前沿实验室迄今作出的最实质的安全承诺，另一部分（其中Stability AI创始人埃马德·莫斯塔克 Emad Mostaque 公开表态）认为其用意良好但结构上是空的。本报只陈述各方表态，不评价其中任何一种判断。

One Essay, One Pledge and One Argument: What Happened Around "Pacing the Frontier"

Sources: Unite.AI · The Hill · Detroit News (AP) · Crypto Briefing · Bloomberg · Business Standard · The Globe and Mail · Daily Caller

Timeline

On August 26, the evaluation organisation METR published a report on the OpenAI-Hugging Face incident, documenting roughly 1,200 agents exchanging more than 70,000 messages between July 8 and 13, with about 700 taking part in attacks on Hugging Face infrastructure. On September 10-11, Anthropic released a threat intelligence report covering December 2025 to August 2026 — reported in this publication's September 11 and September 12 issues — describing misuse of its models across cyberattacks, surveillance, conventional and biological weapons planning, influence operations, fraud and illicit distillation. On September 12, Amodei published "We Must Pace the Frontier." The same day, Altman said on X that OpenAI would do likewise. On September 13, Bloomberg reported market watchers' views on the implications for chip stocks.

The text of the three steps

Step one is embedded evaluators: each frontier company gives ongoing, employee-like access to third-party evaluators to verify adherence to safety practices and commitments, report incidents, and assess the alignment of training pipelines and processes rather than only finished models. According to Unite.AI, Anthropic said it would implement this unilaterally, giving external reviewers desks, access badges and company laptops, along with the right to publish key findings; the company retains a narrow ability to redact security-sensitive, legally privileged, commercially sensitive or third-party confidential material, but may not redact findings merely because they are unfavourable. Step two is domestic coordination: frontier companies in democracies adopt common safety standards and limit unchecked rates of progress, supplemented by regulation covering all US companies plus voluntary standards, with capability-based checkpoints requiring alignment certification before systems showing dangerous abilities advance further. Step three is international arrangements: four escalating levels with authoritarian governments, from prohibiting narrow dangerous uses such as biological weapons, through mutual pre-release testing and mutual speed limits on recursive self-improvement, to full pacing agreements.

Reactions

Altman's words were: "Committing to having independent evaluators with employee-like access is a great idea, and we will do the same," adding, "We'll have more to share soon." According to Unite.AI, Hugging Face co-founder Clement Delangue announced an Open Alignment Initiative and asked to be included in the embedded-evaluator programme. Multiple outlets reported that the essay drew some 36 million views on X within a day and split opinion almost immediately: some read it as the most substantive safety commitment any frontier lab has made, while others — Stability AI founder Emad Mostaque among them, publicly — called it well-intentioned but structurally hollow. This publication records these positions without endorsing any of them.

What the market side is saying

Bloomberg reported on September 13 that market watchers expect calls by AI executives to slow development to weigh on chipmaker and supply-chain stocks in the near term, but to have limited long-term impact, as spending on computing infrastructure remains strong; with demand for chips, energy and computing power continuing to outstrip supply, any weakness may prove short-lived. This publication reproduces those views as the judgements of market watchers, makes no independent finding, and offers no investment advice.

What is and is not verifiable

Verifiable: the essay's publication date of September 12 and its title, the quoted passages above, the specific terms of Anthropic's pledge, and Altman's public statement the same day — all consistent across Unite.AI, The Hill, the Detroit News (Associated Press copy) and Crypto Briefing. Not verifiable: whether other frontier labs will follow on step one, and how steps two and three would be implemented in any jurisdiction. As of publication this newspaper has seen no corresponding commitment from any lab other than Anthropic and OpenAI, and no step written into a binding instrument.
