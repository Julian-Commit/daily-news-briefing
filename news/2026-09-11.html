Hong Kong Court Sentences Tiananmen Vigil Organisers: Seven Years for Lee Cheuk-yan, Seven Years Three Months for Chow Hang-tung, Five Years Two Months for Albert Ho

Al Jazeera · NPR · NBC News · Reuters · AP News · 2026-09-11

A Hong Kong court passed sentence on September 11 (Hong Kong time) in the incitement-to-subversion case against the Hong Kong Alliance in Support of Patriotic Democratic Movements of China. Lee Cheuk-yan, 69, a former chair of the alliance and former legislator, received seven years; Chow Hang-tung, 41, a barrister and the alliance's former vice-chair, received seven years and three months; and Albert Ho, 74, another former chair and a Democratic Party figure who had pleaded guilty, received five years and two months. The now-disbanded alliance was fined HK$1.5 million (about $191,000). The charge was incitement to subversion under the national security law Beijing imposed in 2020. The judges held that the alliance's call to “end one-party dictatorship” amounted to inciting others to overthrow the Chinese Communist Party. The alliance had organised candlelight vigils in Victoria Park each year for three decades from 1990 to mark the 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown. The three defendants have been in custody since 2021; the trial opened in January 2026, and according to NBC News they were convicted on August 21, 2026. Chow, who represented herself, said the verdict “boils down to saying that pursuing democracy is a crime” and that she would “rather be a criminal than a person who betrays their conscience.” Lee's wife, Elizabeth Tang, said he would “definitely appeal.” Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council condemned the sentences, saying that “pursuing democracy and human rights is not a crime” and accusing the authorities of conducting “political trials in the name of national security.” United Nations bodies, rights groups and several foreign governments had previously objected to the convictions. US Supreme Court Blocks Missouri's New Congressional Map for the Second Time in a Week; the 2022 Map Will Apply in November

NBC News · CBS News · The Washington Post · Al Jazeera · PBS News · 2026-09-10

The US Supreme Court on September 10 issued an unsigned order temporarily blocking Missouri from using a newly drawn congressional map in the November midterm elections, the second such order from the court this week. No dissents were noted. Under the order, Missouri's secretary of state, Denny Hoskins, must use the state's 2022 congressional map in November. Under the 2022 map, Missouri's House delegation consists of six Republicans and two Democrats; the blocked map targeted the seat long held by Democrat Emanuel Cleaver and could have produced a 7-1 split. The emergency application was brought by a group called People Not Politicians, led by the activist Richard von Glahn. A separate procedural thread concerns a state referendum: opponents gathered signatures to put the map to a vote, and Hoskins delayed ruling on whether it would appear on the ballot before rejecting it, arguing that redistricting plans are not subject to voter approval; the Missouri Supreme Court disagreed and allowed the referendum to proceed. A federal lawsuit in the matter was filed by Rick Brattin, Republican Representative Bob Onder and two voters. On background, the map was enacted by Missouri Republicans in 2025 as part of a nationwide mid-decade redistricting effort pressed by President Donald Trump. Justice Department Said to Be Examining Whether Nvidia's Groq Licensing Deal Was Structured to Avoid Merger Review

Bloomberg · The New York Times · Reuters · The Next Web · DigiTimes · 2026-09-10

The New York Times reported on September 10, with Bloomberg and Reuters following, that the US Justice Department is investigating a transaction between Nvidia and the AI chip company Groq, examining whether Nvidia deliberately structured it as a licensing agreement to avoid routine government scrutiny of mergers and acquisitions. Under the structure, Nvidia took a non-exclusive licence to Groq's chip technology and hired several executives including the founder, Jonathan Ross, but did not buy the company. Reported values differ between outlets: The Next Web and PC Gamer put the figure at $20 billion and Reuters at $17 billion, a gap that may turn on whether the hiring arrangement is counted. On the timeline, the deal was announced in December, and the Justice Department opened its inquiry shortly afterwards and has since sent Nvidia a formal request for information. On possible outcomes, reporting notes that investigators could seek fines if they find wrongdoing, that forcing Nvidia to unwind the deal is considered unlikely, and that the inquiry could end without enforcement action. An Nvidia spokesperson said: “The Groq story is a prime example of the American system working as designed to promote innovation, reward entrepreneurs, and benefit consumers.” No public comment from the Justice Department or Groq had been reported at the time of writing. 一个口号、三十年集会与五年羁押：支联会案的时间线

综合信源：Al Jazeera · NPR · NBC News · Reuters（经 Daily Maverick） · AP News（经 WTOP） · BusinessWorld · GlobalPost

时间线：1989 年至 2026 年 9 月 11 日

1989 年 5 月，香港市民支援爱国民主运动联合会（支联会）成立。1990 年起，该组织每年在香港维多利亚公园举办六四烛光悼念晚会，连续三十年。2020 年 6 月 30 日，《香港国家安全法》生效，设分裂国家、颠覆国家政权、恐怖活动与勾结外国势力四类罪行。2021 年，李卓人、邹幸彤与何俊仁等人被控煽动颠覆国家政权并被还押；同年 9 月，支联会会员大会通过解散。2026 年 1 月，本案开庭审理。据 NBC 新闻，三人于 2026 年 8 月 21 日被裁定罪名成立。2026 年 9 月 11 日（香港时间）宣判：李卓人 7 年、邹幸彤 7 年 3 个月、何俊仁 5 年 2 个月，支联会本身被判罚款 150 万港元。

争议焦点：口号的法律属性

据多家报道，法官认定支联会长期使用的「结束一党专政」这一主张构成煽动他人推翻中国共产党领导的政权。这是本案中控辩双方分歧最直接的一点：控方将该口号视为颠覆意图的证据，被告方则主张其属于政治表达。邹幸彤在庭上自行辩护，称判决「归结起来就是说追求民主是犯罪」。何俊仁在三人中刑期最短，此前已认罪。

同期另一条线：黄之锋案

据 GlobalPost 报道，2026 年 9 月 3 日，香港民主派人士黄之锋在其第二宗国安案件中就「串谋勾结外国势力」罪名认罪，面临可能的终身监禁；控方指其在 2020 年请求外国实体对中国与香港「实施制裁或封锁」。该案与支联会案彼此独立，但审理节点相隔仅一周。

外部反应

Sources: ABC News (Australia) · CNN · Mongabay · NASA Earth Observatory · The Nation Thailand · Indonesia's Ministry of Forestry · Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency (BMKG)

Timeline: June to September 2026

In July, Mongabay reported elevated fire hotspot activity in Indonesia. On August 18 Indonesia's forestry minister said 202,000 hectares of forest and land had burned nationwide — a figure that, according to The Nation Thailand, did not yet include the whole of August. During August, 72 people were arrested on suspicion of starting fires. From August 30 to September 2, air quality in Manila entered the “acutely unhealthy” range. In the last week of August, asthma cases in Malaysia rose 259%. On September 1, NASA's Aqua satellite captured the haze over Indonesia. The ABC reported from the ground on September 7, and CNN reported on the link between the strong El Nino and the peatland fires on September 9.

The numbers: visibility, air quality and hospital visits

According to the ABC, the air quality index in Pontianak, West Kalimantan, exceeded 900 at one point — three times the “hazardous” threshold — and visibility in affected areas fell to between 200 and 500 metres. The meteorologist Septika Sari said: “We can see from our PM 2.5 data, it has now entered the dangerous category … in terms of colour on the graph, it has gone to black.” Indonesia's health ministry has recorded tens of thousands of fire-linked respiratory infections. The health official Dr Muhammad Arief said: “Compared to previous years, this year the number has roughly doubled from the year before.” Some residents reported having seen no rain for more than 60 days.

Why peatland matters

The worst-hit areas are Kalimantan and Sumatra. After prolonged drainage and drought, fire in peat can smoulder below the surface, where conventional firefighting struggles to reach it, while releasing large volumes of smoke particulates that travel across borders as haze. That is why a single fire season becomes an air quality problem in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines at once. Malaysia's Sarawak state has declared an emergency, and visibility in Kuala Lumpur has fallen sharply.

Comparison with 2015

2015 was Indonesia's worst recent fire season, with roughly 2.6 million hectares burned. At this stage the assessments of people on the ground and the aggregate data do not yet line up: the firefighter Andri Susanto told the ABC that “conditions in 2026 are indeed worse than in 2015,” while the published figure of 202,000 hectares runs only to mid-August and is far below the 2015 season total. The two are not necessarily in conflict — one describes local intensity and rate of spread, the other is a running total for a season that is not over.

What is and is not verifiable

Verifiable: the 202,000-hectare figure published by Indonesia's forestry ministry on August 18; the September 1 satellite imagery released by NASA Earth Observatory; the emergency declaration in Sarawak; and the named quotations in the ABC's on-the-ground reporting. Not verifiable: the final burned area for the season, which is still under way; a complete quantification of the transboundary haze's health effects (Malaysia's 259% rise in asthma cases is a single week of regional data, not a national figure); and the cause of every individual fire — 72 arrests establish that investigations into suspected arson exist, not that the cause of all hotspots has been determined.
