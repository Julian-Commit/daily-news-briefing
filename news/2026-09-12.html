Houthi Forces Take Mayun Island Inside the Bab el-Mandeb; the Whole of Yemen's Red Sea Coastline Changes Hands

NPR · Euronews · Al Jazeera · NBC News · 2026-09-12

NPR reported that Yemen's Houthi forces took control on September 12 of Mayun Island, also known as Perim, inside the Bab el-Mandeb Strait; a senior military official with Yemen's internationally recognised government and a Houthi official both confirmed the capture. Euronews reported that Houthi fighters advanced onto the island after government forces withdrew. Mayun is a volcanic island of about 13 square kilometres at the narrowest part of the strait, dividing it into two shipping lanes, according to Al Jazeera. The sequence of the advance was: on September 10 the Houthis took Zuqar, the largest island of the Red Sea archipelago, and attacked Mokha; on September 11 they took the port of Mokha, the town of Dhubab and the village of Murad; and on September 12 they took Mayun. According to Euronews, the offensive has killed more than 500 people, the UN migration agency counts roughly 46,000 displaced, and more than 2,000 government troops are besieged on the Hanish islands. NPR reported that Saudi Arabia shut down a major oil pipeline as a precaution after it came under attack. On shipping, Euronews noted that nearly one-third of the world's container traffic passes through the Red Sea, and that the Strait of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb together account for more than 25 per cent of seaborne crude oil and petroleum product trade. Al Jazeera quoted Caroline Rose, a senior director at the Soufan Center, as saying the group now “has the real capability to shut down any maritime traffic through Bab al-Mandeb if they want to.” Cross-verified: NPR · Euronews · Al Jazeera · NBC News

BirdLife's State of the World's Birds 2026: 45 Per Cent of 1,843 Migratory Species Are Declining and 200 Are Threatened With Extinction

BirdLife International · Mongabay · The Express Tribune · EnviroNews · Mail & Guardian · 2026-09-11

BirdLife International released State of the World's Birds 2026 on September 11 at the Global Flyways Summit in Nairobi, Kenya. It is the sixth edition of the series and the first devoted to migratory birds and the flyways they depend on. Of the 1,843 migratory species assessed, 45 per cent have declining global populations, 30 per cent are stable, 14 per cent are increasing and the trends of the remaining 11 per cent are unknown. Two hundred species, about 11 per cent of the 1,843, are threatened with extinction: 32 classified as critically endangered, 57 as endangered and 111 as vulnerable. The steepest declines were recorded among species dependent on marine and coastal wetlands; among the four major land-based flyway systems, the East Asian-Australasian Flyway had the highest proportion of declining species, at 53 per cent. On threats, invasive species and disease affect 139 threatened species, agriculture and climate change 103 each, hunting and trapping 99, and pollution 96. According to Mongabay, only 9 per cent of migratory species are adequately protected across their full annual cycle, against 45 per cent of non-migratory species. The report calls for stronger international cooperation to protect and restore habitats, prevent illegal killing, reduce seabird deaths caused by fishing and control invasive species. 《环境研究快报》研究：冻土、湿地、内陆水体与野火的增温反馈，可能使本世纪升温再增0.2至0.4摄氏度

Environmental Research Letters · Phys.org · CBC News · Gizmodo · EurekAlert · 2026-09-10

一项于9月10日发表在《环境研究快报》（Environmental Research Letters）第21卷第17期的研究测算，由升温诱发的自然源温室气体排放，可能在本世纪内额外带来约0.2至0.4摄氏度（0.4至0.7华氏度）的升温，相当于将2020年以后的人为升温放大20%至30%。论文题为「Projected amplification of global warming by warming-induced greenhouse gas emissions」。据Phys.org与EurekAlert，该研究是迄今将四类主要反馈——多年冻土融化、野火、湿地与内陆水体——合并量化的最大规模尝试。机制包括：升温后湿地与湖泊释放甲烷，多年冻土融化释放二氧化碳与甲烷，以及气候驱动的野火烧掉森林中储存的碳。作者指出，这些排放在当前大多数气候模型与情景设定中尚未得到表达，并表示更快的减排会降低这类反馈的风险。据CBC与Gizmodo，论文作者来自斯坦福大学、Spark Climate Solutions、美国环境保护基金会（Environmental Defense Fund）与Woodwell气候研究中心等机构。交叉核验：Environmental Research Letters（IOPscience） · Phys.org · CBC News · Gizmodo · EurekAlert

Study in Environmental Research Letters: Feedbacks From Permafrost, Wetlands, Inland Waters and Wildfire Could Add 0.2 to 0.4C of Warming This Century

Environmental Research Letters · Phys.org · CBC News · Gizmodo · EurekAlert · 2026-09-10

A study published on September 10 in Environmental Research Letters, Volume 21, Number 17, estimates that warming-induced greenhouse gas emissions from natural sources could add roughly 0.2 to 0.4 degrees Celsius (0.4 to 0.7 degrees Fahrenheit) of warming this century, amplifying post-2020 anthropogenic warming by 20 to 30 per cent. The paper is titled “Projected amplification of global warming by warming-induced greenhouse gas emissions.” According to Phys.org and EurekAlert, it is the largest effort to date to quantify the combined effect of four major feedbacks: permafrost thaw, wildfires, wetlands and inland waters. The mechanisms include methane released by warming wetlands and lakes, carbon dioxide and methane released by thawing permafrost, and emissions from climate-driven wildfires burning carbon stored in forests. The authors say these emissions are largely unrepresented in current climate models and scenarios, and that faster emissions reductions would reduce the feedback risk. According to CBC and Gizmodo, the authors are affiliated with institutions including Stanford University, Spark Climate Solutions, the Environmental Defense Fund and the Woodwell Climate Research Center. 十一个成员、四分之一的GDP与一份协商一致的文件：新德里金砖峰会的时间线与文本

综合信源：The National · The Tribune（India） · ANI · The Statesman · The News Mill · LatestLY · BRICS Information Centre（多伦多大学）

时间线：从喀山到新德里

2024年10月，金砖第十六次峰会在俄罗斯喀山举行。2025年7月6日至7日，第十七次峰会在巴西里约热内卢举行，发布《里约热内卢宣言》（文本见多伦多大学金砖信息中心存档）。2026年由印度担任主席国，峰会主题为「Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability」。2026年9月12日，第十八次峰会在新德里举行，一致通过《新德里宣言》；据报道，9月13日另安排了一场面向伙伴国与外延邀请国的公开会议。

文本写了什么

中东部分：宣言对「中东／西亚地区紧张局势的持续升级」表达深切关切，呼吁「最大限度克制」与避免恶化局势的行动，要求保护平民与民用基础设施，并主张更多运用对话与外交。核设施部分：对「故意攻击民用基础设施及处于国际原子能机构全面保障监督下的和平核设施」表示严重关切，并称核保障监督、核安全与核安保「必须始终得到维护，包括在武装冲突中」。核裁军部分：据ANI与《政治家报》（The Statesman），宣言对核冲突风险上升表示关切，呼吁加强裁军、军备控制与核不扩散方面的努力，重申支持无核武器区，并强调加快建立中东无核武器及其他大规模杀伤性武器区的进程。此外，宣言还涉及谴责恐怖主义、多边主义、全球南方的代表性与和平解决争端。

背景：扩员后的体量与当下的市场条件

据《民族报》，金砖机制已从巴西、俄罗斯、印度、中国、南非五国扩展到现在的11个成员国，合计约占全球名义GDP的25%与全球人口的约50%。本次峰会召开时的市场背景是：国际油价处于每桶100美元上方，霍尔木兹海峡航运接近停滞。本报9月10日那期已报道，布伦特原油9月9日结算价报每桶101.21美元，为5月22日以来最高。

领导人说了什么

据《民族报》，印度总理莫迪在峰会上提出要把「特权的金字塔」转变为「伙伴关系的平台」；中国国家主席习近平称中东战事有悖于「国际社会的共同利益」。本报不对上述表述作出评价，仅引述其原文口径。

可核验与不可核验

可核验的是：宣言的通过日期（9月12日）、地点（新德里）与上述引号内文字，在《民族报》《印度论坛报》、ANI与《政治家报》的报道中一致；成员国数量与出席领导人名单有多家报道支持。不可核验的是：宣言的磋商过程。金砖以协商一致方式通过文件，部分媒体提及成员国在中东问题上立场存在差异，但本报未能取得谈判过程的一手材料，因此不就分歧的具体内容作出陈述。

Eleven Members, a Quarter of World GDP and One Consensus Document: A Timeline and Text Reading of the New Delhi BRICS Summit

Sources: The National · The Tribune (India) · ANI · The Statesman · The News Mill · LatestLY · BRICS Information Centre (University of Toronto)

Timeline: from Kazan to New Delhi

The 16th BRICS summit was held in Kazan, Russia, in October 2024. The 17th was held in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, on July 6-7, 2025, and produced the Rio de Janeiro Declaration, whose text is archived by the BRICS Information Centre at the University of Toronto. India chairs the grouping in 2026 under the theme “Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.” On September 12, 2026, the 18th summit convened in New Delhi and unanimously adopted the New Delhi Declaration; reports indicate that an open session with partner countries and outreach invitees was scheduled for September 13.

What the text says

综合信源：BirdLife International · Mongabay · The Express Tribune · EnviroNews · Mail & Guardian · Time News · Gozo News

一、数据：1,843个物种的一次盘点

国际鸟盟9月11日在内罗毕发布的《世界鸟类状况2026》，是该系列的第六版，也是首次专门处理迁徙鸟类。评估的1,843个迁徙物种中，45%数量下降，30%稳定，14%增长，11%趋势不明。受灭绝威胁的200种中，32种极危、57种濒危、111种易危。在四大陆基迁飞区中，东亚—澳大拉西亚迁飞区的下降物种占比最高，为53%。

二、一个结构性缺口：保护必须覆盖整条路线

据Mongabay，仅有9%的迁徙物种在全年生活史周期（繁殖地、越冬地与沿途停歇地）中均获得充分保护，而非迁徙物种的这一比例为45%。这是迁徙物种与定居物种在保护上的关键区别：一条路线上任何一环失守，整条路线上的保护投入都会被抵消。报告还指出，超过一半已评估的关键生物多样性区域状况较差或极差，Mongabay给出的数字为58%。威胁因素的分布为：入侵物种与疾病影响139个受威胁物种，农业与气候变化各103个，狩猎与诱捕99个，污染96个。

三、宣言与钱：内罗毕峰会上的承诺

会上签署了《内罗毕迁飞路线宣言》（Nairobi Flyways Declaration），签署方包括政府、科学家、非政府组织与金融机构，内容为加强跨境保护合作。资金方面：据Mongabay，亚洲开发银行承诺30亿美元，用于东亚—澳大拉西亚迁飞区的湿地保护；据EnviroNews，自2021年以来开发性金融机构共动员超过60亿美元的保护资金。2026年6月，全球环境基金（GEF）批准了全球迁飞路线赠款机制，提供897万美元直接赠款与1.07亿美元共同融资，用于900万公顷湿地的保护与修复，估计惠及约33万人。

四、一个反向案例

报告并非只有下降曲线。据Mongabay，埃及秃鹫在巴尔干地区的种群在一项国际保护行动后出现回升。报告提出的措施清单包括：保护与修复栖息地、制止非法杀戮、减少渔业造成的海鸟死亡、控制入侵物种，以及使农业、林业与基础设施对野生动物更安全。

五、可核验与不可核验

可核验的是：45%、30%、14%、11%四项相加为100%；32、57与111相加为200，与「约11%」的表述一致；上述数字在国际鸟盟发布稿与多家转载报道中一致。需要区分的是：Mongabay另提到一个与「11%」并列的绝对数1,256种，但该数字适用的是国际自然保护联盟红色名录上超过11,000种鸟类的整体，而非本报告专门评估的迁徙物种，两者不应混用。不可核验的是：本报未能直接读取报告全文PDF（国际鸟盟站点对本报的请求返回403），上述数据均来自其发布稿与各媒体的转述。

Three Things on One Flyway: The Data, the Declaration and the Money

Sources: BirdLife International · Mongabay · The Express Tribune · EnviroNews · Mail & Guardian · Time News · Gozo News

1. The data: a stocktake of 1,843 species

State of the World's Birds 2026, released by BirdLife International in Nairobi on September 11, is the sixth edition of the series and the first devoted to migratory birds. Of the 1,843 migratory species assessed, 45 per cent are declining, 30 per cent are stable, 14 per cent are increasing and 11 per cent have unknown trends. Of the 200 species threatened with extinction, 32 are critically endangered, 57 endangered and 111 vulnerable. Among the four major land-based flyway systems, the East Asian-Australasian Flyway has the highest share of declining species, at 53 per cent.

2. A structural gap: protection has to cover the whole route

According to Mongabay, only 9 per cent of migratory species are adequately protected across their full annual cycle — breeding grounds, wintering grounds and stopover sites — against 45 per cent of non-migratory species. This is the key difference between migratory and resident birds in conservation terms: a gap at any one link on a route cancels out protection spending along the rest of it. The report also finds that more than half of assessed Key Biodiversity Areas are in poor or very poor condition, a figure Mongabay puts at 58 per cent. The distribution of threats: invasive species and disease affect 139 threatened species, agriculture and climate change 103 each, hunting and trapping 99, and pollution 96.

3. The declaration and the money: commitments at the Nairobi summit

The Nairobi Flyways Declaration was signed at the summit by governments, scientists, NGOs and financial institutions, committing them to stronger cross-border conservation cooperation. On finance: according to Mongabay, the Asian Development Bank pledged $3 billion toward wetland protection in the East Asian-Australasian Flyway; according to EnviroNews, development banks have mobilised more than $6 billion in conservation finance since 2021. In June 2026 the Global Environment Facility approved the Global Flyways Grant Mechanism, providing $8.97 million in direct grants and $107 million in co-financing for conservation and restoration across nine million hectares of wetlands, benefiting an estimated 330,000 people.

4. One case running the other way

The report is not only declining curves. According to Mongabay, the Egyptian vulture population in the Balkans has been recovering following an international conservation effort. The measures the report proposes include protecting and restoring habitats, preventing illegal killing, reducing seabird deaths caused by fishing, controlling invasive species, and making agriculture, forestry and infrastructure safer for wildlife.

5. What is and is not verifiable

Verifiable: 45, 30, 14 and 11 per cent sum to 100; 32 plus 57 plus 111 equals 200, consistent with the “about 11 per cent” description; and these figures are consistent across BirdLife's own release and multiple secondary reports. A distinction worth drawing: Mongabay also cites an absolute figure of 1,256 species alongside an 11 per cent share, but that number applies to the more than 11,000 bird species on the IUCN Red List as a whole, not to the migratory species assessed in this report; the two should not be conflated. Not verifiable: this publication could not read the full report PDF directly — BirdLife's site returned a 403 to our request — so the data above come from its press release and from secondary reporting.
