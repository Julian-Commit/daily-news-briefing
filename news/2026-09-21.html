这篇解读做什么

把2026年9月20日德国梅克伦堡-前波美拉尼亚州与柏林两场州议会选举中，从投票当晚的出口民调到次日计票数字之间的变化按顺序排列，并标注每一条的来源。本报不预测组阁，不评价任何政党、候选人或选民，也不对德国政治走向作出判断。

本报上一期记录了什么

本报第016期（9月20日）记录的是当晚公布的出口民调与推算值：据法国国际广播电视台，梅前州德国选择党（AfD）37%、社会民主党（SPD）35.5%、基督教民主联盟（CDU）5.5%；NBC 新闻同日给出的基民盟数字为5%。柏林方面为左翼党（Die Linke）24.5%、基民盟20%、德国选择党16%。本报当时明确写明这些是出口民调与推算值，未见最终计票，也未见投票率数据。

计票之后的梅前州数字

据公开汇总的该州选举结果：德国选择党388,026票、38.2%、32席，较2021年增18席；社会民主党360,393票、35.5%、29席，减5席；左翼党66,388票、6.5%、5席，减4席；绿党57,587票、5.7%、5席，持平；基民盟49,629票、4.9%、0席，由12席降为0席；萨拉·瓦根克内希特联盟（BSW）49,036票、4.8%、0席，为首次参选；其余政党合计4.4%。州议会共71席，过半数需36席。

被0.1个百分点分开的两件事

梅前州适用5%的议席分配门槛。基民盟的4.9%与 BSW 的4.8%均低于该门槛，两党均未获得席位；左翼党的6.5%与绿党的5.7%在门槛之上，各获5席。就基民盟而言，据美联社与 CNBC 的报道，这是该党战后在德国任一州选举中的最低结果；据公开汇总的结果说明，这也是基民盟首次在德国州选举中未取得席位。本报在此只陈述门槛规则与已公布的数字，不推断若干分点之差的成因。

据公开汇总的选举数据，梅前州本次总投票数为1,024,251张，投票率78.1%，较2021年的同项数据上升7.3个百分点。本报第016期截稿时未见任一州的投票率数据，本期补记这一项。

据路透社（经 Just Security 转述），柏林方面左翼党得票25%居首，基民盟19%位居第二；该报道称这是左翼党首次在州一级选举中居首。另据检索到的公开数据，德国选择党在柏林得票16.3%。与当晚出口民调（左翼党24.5%、基民盟20%、德国选择党16%）相比，三项数字均有小幅变动。需要说明：截至本报截稿，本报未见柏林州选举的完整席位分配与投票率数据。

当事人表态与党内程序

据 CNN，默茨称选举结果是一场「灾难」；据美联社（经 NPR 刊发）与 CNBC，他同时表示不会退出政府的改革议程。据彭博社，默茨将于周一上午与基民盟领导层会面。背景方面，据 CNBC，基民盟在9月6日萨克森-安哈尔特州选举中败于德国选择党，此前已对默茨的党内权威构成影响。

From Exit Polls to the Count: Two Outcomes Separated by a Tenth of a Point in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, and a Turnout Figure Up 7.3 Points

Sources: Reuters (via U.S. News and Just Security) · The Associated Press (via NPR) · CNN · CNBC · Bloomberg · France 24 · Compiled election results

What this piece does

It sets out, in order and with the source of each item marked, what changed between the exit polls on the evening of September 20, 2026 and the counted figures published the following day in the two German state elections, in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Berlin. This publication makes no prediction about coalition formation, assesses no party, candidate or voter, and offers no judgement about the direction of German politics.

What this publication recorded last issue

Issue No.016, of September 20, recorded the exit polls and projections published that evening: per France 24, the AfD on 37%, the SPD on 35.5% and the CDU on 5.5% in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, with NBC News giving a CDU figure of 5% the same day; and in Berlin, Die Linke on 24.5%, the CDU on 20% and the AfD on 16%. That issue stated explicitly that these were exit polls and projections, that no final count had been seen and that no turnout data were available.

The Mecklenburg-Vorpommern figures after the count

Compiled results for the state record: the AfD on 388,026 votes, 38.2% and 32 seats, up 18 on 2021; the SPD on 360,393 votes, 35.5% and 29 seats, down 5; Die Linke on 66,388 votes, 6.5% and 5 seats, down 4; the Greens on 57,587 votes, 5.7% and 5 seats, unchanged; the CDU on 49,629 votes, 4.9% and no seats, down from 12; the BSW on 49,036 votes, 4.8% and no seats, contesting for the first time; and other parties on 4.4% combined. The Landtag has 71 seats, with 36 needed for a majority.

Two outcomes separated by a tenth of a point

Mecklenburg-Vorpommern applies a 5% threshold for the allocation of seats. The CDU on 4.9% and the BSW on 4.8% both fell below it and took no seats; Die Linke on 6.5% and the Greens on 5.7% cleared it and took five each. For the CDU, the Associated Press and CNBC report that this is the party’s lowest result in any German state election since the Second World War, and the compiled results note that it is the first time the CDU has taken no seats in a German state election. This publication states the threshold rule and the published figures, and draws no inference about the causes of a margin of a few tenths of a point.

Turnout

Compiled election data record 1,024,251 votes cast in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, for a turnout of 78.1%, up 7.3 percentage points on the same figure in 2021. Issue No.016 had seen no turnout data for either state at its deadline; this issue records that figure.

Berlin

Reuters, as relayed by Just Security, reports Die Linke first in Berlin on 25% and the CDU second on 19%, and describes this as the first time Die Linke has come first at state level. Other published data record the AfD on 16.3% there. Compared with the exit polls of that evening (Die Linke 24.5%, CDU 20%, AfD 16%), all three figures moved slightly. A qualification: as of this issue’s deadline, this publication has seen neither a full seat allocation nor a turnout figure for the Berlin election.

Statements and party process

CNN reports that Merz called the result a disaster; the Associated Press (via NPR) and CNBC report that he simultaneously rejected any retreat from his government’s reform agenda. Bloomberg reports that Merz was to meet the CDU leadership on Monday morning. As background, CNBC notes that the CDU’s defeat to the AfD in the Saxony-Anhalt election of September 6 had already affected Merz’s standing within his party.

What is known and what is not

Known: the polling dates in both states, the counted figures and seat allocation in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, that state’s turnout, part of the Berlin vote shares, Merz’s public statements and the Monday party meeting. Not known: the full seat allocation and turnout in Berlin, the coalition talks in either state, and the outcome of the CDU leadership meeting. This publication will continue to record these once official data and arrangements are published.
