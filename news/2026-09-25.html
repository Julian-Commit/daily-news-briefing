On September 25 the general debate of the 81st UN General Assembly entered its fourth day, with diplomacy and war running in parallel inside the same building. Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi set out in New York a seven-day proposal conveyed to Washington through intermediaries: if the United States halts military action on all fronts, lifts its naval blockade and oil sanctions and releases frozen assets, the Strait of Hormuz would reopen at the end of the seventh day, with nuclear talks resuming afterwards. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said it would be wrong to describe the related meeting as a major breakthrough. A day earlier, delegations from many countries walked out as Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu addressed the Assembly, where he called allegations of genocide in Gaza "the biggest lie of the century." Also on September 24, Chinese President Xi Jinping completed bilateral talks and a state dinner at the White House, with artificial intelligence on the published agenda and no joint document issued; he left the United States on September 25 after tea and a tour of the National Archives. In technology, Google confirmed it will send four Tensor Processing Units into low Earth orbit on October 1, the first field test of the idea of moving data centres into space. In the environment, Hurricane Nolo approached Hawaii's Big Island, with the US National Hurricane Center warning of possible "catastrophic life-threatening flooding and mudslides," while Nepal's Prime Minister Balendra Shah brought his country's September glacier-burst floods to the UN podium and asked for recovery finance in grants rather than loans. This briefing records each account against its own source rather than choosing between them, and does not evaluate any party's claims.

谷歌将于10月1日把4枚 TPU 送入近地轨道：冰箱大小的「MVP」卫星由 Planet Labs 制造，搭乘 SpaceX Transporter-18 拼车发射

据 SiliconANGLE 9月24日报道与 Data Center Dynamics 报道，谷歌将于10月1日发射一颗搭载4枚自研张量处理器（TPU）的原型卫星进入近地轨道，这是其「Project Suncatcher」研究项目的首次在轨试验。据 SiliconANGLE，该卫星名为「MVP」，体积约与一台冰箱相当，由卫星成像公司 Planet Labs PBC 制造。据 Artificially Intimidating 9月25日援引谷歌官方博客与《纽约时报》，发射地点为加利福尼亚州范登堡太空军基地，运载工具为 SpaceX 的猎鹰9号火箭；据 Hardware Busters，该星搭乘的是 Transporter-18 拼车任务，进入太阳同步轨道。技术参数方面，据 SiliconANGLE：卫星太阳能板发电功率约1千瓦；受散热条件限制，星上 TPU 只能以约15分钟为一段执行计算任务，随后须停机降温。测试目标方面，据 SiliconANGLE，本次任务检验四件事：TPU 能否承受发射过程的剧烈受力、芯片对宇宙辐射与太阳活动的耐受性、真空环境下散热系统的有效性，以及辐射条件下的数据完整性。据 SiliconANGLE 援引项目方表述，「这次首发是为了看清什么能用、找出失效点」，并把结论用于后续任务。后续安排方面，据 SiliconANGLE，2027年计划发射两颗卫星，测试高性能激光通信链路；据 Data Center Dynamics，谷歌设想的长期形态是由81颗卫星组成、跨度约1公里的在轨计算集群。经济性方面，据 Artificially Intimidating 援引谷歌官方博客，该设想成立的前提之一是发射成本降至每千克200美元以下，谷歌预计这一水平可能在2030年代中期出现；同一来源称，太阳同步轨道上太阳能板的发电效率最高可达地面的8倍。本报只记录已公布的任务参数与时间安排，不预测试验结果。

Google Will Send Four TPUs Into Low Earth Orbit on October 1: the Refrigerator-Sized “MVP” Satellite Was Built by Planet Labs and Rides SpaceX’s Transporter-18

SiliconANGLE · Data Center Dynamics · Hardware Busters · Inc. · Artificially Intimidating (citing Google’s official blog and The New York Times) · September 24–25, 2026

SiliconANGLE reported on September 24, as did Data Center Dynamics, that Google will launch a prototype satellite carrying four of its custom Tensor Processing Units into low Earth orbit on October 1, the first in-orbit test of its Project Suncatcher research effort. According to SiliconANGLE the satellite is called MVP, is roughly the size of a refrigerator, and was built by the satellite imaging company Planet Labs PBC. Artificially Intimidating, citing Google’s official blog and The New York Times, reported on September 25 that the launch site is Vandenberg Space Force Base in California and the vehicle is a SpaceX Falcon 9; Hardware Busters reported that the satellite flies on the Transporter-18 rideshare mission into a sun-synchronous orbit. On specifications, SiliconANGLE reported that the satellite’s solar panels generate about one kilowatt, and that because of cooling limits the TPUs can run workloads only in bursts of about 15 minutes before shutting down to cool. On what the mission measures, SiliconANGLE listed four things: whether the TPUs survive the forces of launch, how the chips withstand cosmic radiation and solar events, how effectively the cooling system works in vacuum, and data integrity under radiation. SiliconANGLE quoted the project as saying that “this first launch is about seeing what works, identifying points of failure,” with the findings applied to later missions. On what comes next, SiliconANGLE reported that two satellites are planned for 2027 to test high-performance laser communication links, and Data Center Dynamics reported that Google’s longer-term concept is an in-orbit compute cluster of 81 satellites spanning roughly one kilometre. On economics, Artificially Intimidating, citing Google’s blog, reported that the concept depends in part on launch costs falling below $200 per kilogram, a level Google expects could arrive in the mid-2030s, and that solar panels in a sun-synchronous orbit can be up to eight times as productive as on Earth. This briefing records the published mission parameters and schedule and does not forecast the result of the test.
