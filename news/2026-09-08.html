UK Bans Imports from Israeli West Bank Settlements, Effective Within Six to Nine Months; Israeli and US Officials Respond

NPR · BBC · Reuters · Al Jazeera · NBC News · AP · 2026-09-08

UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband told the House of Commons on September 8 that Britain will ban imports of all goods produced in Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, and restrict services to settlements including financing, construction, infrastructure, real estate and advertising. Foreign Minister Gideon Saar said "if Britain acts against Israel, Israel will act against Britain." Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich called for the British ambassador in Tel Aviv to be expelled. US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee called the move "an irrational action" and "discrimination against the Jewish people," warning of possible "repercussions or retaliations." France and Canada are also reported to be moving to ban trade in settlement goods, while Ireland, Spain, Norway, Belgium and the Netherlands have already taken or are taking similar steps. 世界气象组织：极端野火烟雾事件自1990年代以来增至三倍，热浪推高地面臭氧

WMO · Reuters · AFP · Phys.org · PreventionWeb · 2026-09-07

世界气象组织（WMO）9月7日发布年度《空气质量与气候公报》。公报援引一项研究指出，全球极端火灾烟雾事件自1990年代以来已增至三倍，在2010年至2018年间每年造成近10万例额外死亡；由于野火烟雾的毒性，现有死亡率模型对相关死亡的低估幅度可能高达93%。WMO指出，欧洲与北美的法规已降低工业与交通来源的PM2.5排放，但与火灾相关的PM2.5暴露反而上升。地面臭氧方面，公报指出气温升高、大气停滞与强太阳辐射会促进臭氧生成，美国东南部、欧洲与中国的热浪期间研究均观察到这一现象，可能导致数万例额外过早死亡，其中以呼吸系统疾病为主。公报同时指出，黑碳与微塑料等物质缺乏充分监测——陆地上的微塑料估计为5,100万吨，海洋中为2,200万吨。公报表示：「由于极端火险天气预计将增加，达到世界卫生组织空气质量准则值将变得越来越困难。」交叉核验：WMO 官方公报 · Reuters · AFP（Dawn / Oman Observer）· Phys.org · PreventionWeb

WMO: Extreme Fire-Smoke Events Have Tripled Since the 1990s, and Heatwaves Are Driving Up Ground-Level Ozone

WMO · Reuters · AFP · Phys.org · PreventionWeb · 2026-09-07

The World Meteorological Organization published its annual Air Quality and Climate Bulletin on September 7. Citing one study, the bulletin says extreme fire-smoke events have tripled globally since the 1990s, contributing to nearly 100,000 additional deaths per year between 2010 and 2018; because of the toxicity of wildfire smoke, existing mortality models may underestimate related deaths by up to 93%. The WMO notes that regulations in Europe and North America have reduced industrial and transport PM2.5 emissions, while exposure to fire-related PM2.5 has increased. On ground-level ozone, the bulletin says rising temperatures, stagnant atmospheric conditions and intense solar radiation promote ozone formation — as observed in studies during heatwaves in the south-eastern United States, Europe and China — with the potential for tens of thousands of additional premature deaths, particularly from respiratory disease. The bulletin also notes that substances such as black carbon and microplastics are insufficiently monitored; microplastics are estimated at 51 million tonnes on land and 22 million tonnes in the oceans. The bulletin states: "Meeting the World Health Organization air quality guidelines will become progressively more difficult, since extreme fire weather is expected to increase." 72小时窗口关闭之后：基辅的这一夜，与仍未成形的三方会谈

综合信源：Al Jazeera · AP News · The Washington Post · CNN · NBC News · Reuters · Axios · POLITICO

时间线：从莫斯科到基辅，再到9月8日凌晨

9月5日，美国总统特使史蒂夫·维特科夫（Steve Witkoff）与贾里德·库什纳（Jared Kushner）在莫斯科与普京会谈逾三小时，这是双方在近八个月僵局后重启接触。9月6日，两人乘火车抵达基辅会见泽连斯基，为特使级别首次访问乌克兰首都。为配合两段行程，俄方同意自9月5日零时起三天不袭击基辅，乌方同意在9月7日之前不袭击莫斯科。这一安排不覆盖前线其他区域。

9月8日凌晨的袭击构成

窗口期结束后的当夜，基辅遭到导弹与无人机袭击。基辅市长维塔利·克利奇科（Vitali Klitschko）通报至少2人死亡、10人受伤。据乌克兰空军数据，防空击落142架无人机、32枚巡航导弹中的31枚以及2枚弹道导弹；俄方发射的弹道导弹总数未披露。达尔尼茨基、戈洛西夫斯基、波迪尔斯基、舍甫琴科夫斯基与索洛缅斯基五个市区，以及市郊的鲍里斯波尔与法斯季夫地区出现损毁。基辅市军事管理局在Telegram通报称，至少16处民用基础设施受损。泽连斯基称此次袭击为复合式打击，动用了弹道导弹、巡航导弹、反舰导弹与「见证者」无人机。乌克兰外长安德烈·瑟比加（Andrii Sybiha）称：「美国总统的和平特使刚一离开，（普京）就对基辅发动了又一次大规模的、可怕的袭击。」

谈判方的表述

维特科夫在基辅表示：「我们进行了非常有意义的讨论，实质、重要，我们受到很大鼓舞。」美方在会后推动举行俄乌美三方会谈，但会谈时间与形式均未宣布。克里姆林宫发言人德米特里·佩斯科夫未排除恢复谈判的可能。泽连斯基在接受Axios采访时表示，美方正在探讨冬季期间的降级措施。克里姆林宫外交政策顾问尤里·乌沙科夫此前称莫斯科会谈「实质、非常坦诚且富有建设性」，并称已形成「一整套和平方案的想法」；普京同时表示双方并未就更广泛的停火达成一致。

同期的两条乌克兰内部与外部消息

乌克兰总检察长鲁斯兰·克拉夫琴科（Ruslan Kravchenko）9月8日提交辞呈。此前乌克兰国家反腐败局（NABU）搜查了总检察长办公室，该办公室国际合作部门副主任谢尔盖·克罗皮瓦（Serhiy Kropyva）被指控经营一个为诈骗性呼叫中心提供庇护的网络。克拉夫琴科否认与该案有关，称辞职是「一个深思熟虑的政治决定」，并表示「我不希望总检察长这一职位被用作政治斗争的工具」。另据POLITICO获得的航运单据，一家中国国有企业向俄罗斯供应了用于自杀式无人机的材料。

目前可核验的是：会谈发生了、临时安排存在过、袭击在窗口期结束后随即发生、伤亡与拦截数字由乌方官方渠道发布。尚未可核验的是：所谓「和平方案的一整套想法」的具体内容，以及三方会谈是否会举行。俄乌双方均未公布任何书面文本。

After the 72-Hour Window Closed: One Night in Kyiv, and Trilateral Talks That Have Yet to Take Shape

Sources: Al Jazeera · AP News · The Washington Post · CNN · NBC News · Reuters · Axios · POLITICO

Timeline: Moscow to Kyiv, then the early hours of September 8

On September 5, US presidential envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Putin in Moscow for more than three hours, resuming contact after nearly eight months of deep freeze. On September 6 they travelled by train to Kyiv to meet Zelenskyy — the first envoy-level visit to the Ukrainian capital. To accommodate both legs, Russia agreed not to strike Kyiv for three days from midnight on September 5, and Ukraine agreed to refrain from strikes on Moscow through September 7. The arrangement did not cover other areas of the front.

On the night the window expired, Kyiv was struck by missiles and drones. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported at least two killed and ten wounded. China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology published its 15th Five-Year Plan for the information and communications industry (2026–2030) on September 7. The report said the two companies might explore other forms of collaboration in future. What they share is a unit of account: compute — measured in euros, EFLOPS, dollars per machine, and board seats.
