The Ferry Virgo Transport 8 Overturns in the Java Sea With 243 Aboard; 129 Still Missing on September 14

Al Jazeera · AP (via NPR) · ABC News · 2026-09-14

According to Al Jazeera, the passenger ferry Virgo Transport 8 left Surabaya in East Java province on September 12 bound for Banjarmasin in South Kalimantan, carrying 243 people — 213 passengers and 30 crew. The Associated Press reported that the captain issued a distress call in the early hours of September 13 saying the vessel was listing in swells of up to three metres; contact was lost at about 2am local time, and roughly two hours later authorities were told the ship had overturned in the Masalembo waters of the Java Sea, about 148 kilometres (92 miles) from Banjarmasin. As of September 14, AP reported at least six dead, 108 rescued and 129 missing; Al Jazeera's September 13 figures were six dead, 107 rescued and 130 missing. I Putu Sudayana, head of the search-and-rescue office in Banjarmasin, told Al Jazeera: "It is extremely risky for vessels. Even our Basarnas vessel, which is 40 metres long, is at great risk." A survivor, bus driver Ahmad Saudi, told AP the ferry appeared overcrowded, with many passengers sleeping in hallways and common areas, and that it listed suddenly without warning. Authorities are examining the weather and possible technical failure; the cause has not been determined. Cross-verified: Al Jazeera · AP · ABC News

The Philippines' Bangsamoro Region Holds Its First Parliamentary Election: 2.39 Million Voters, 80 Seats

Al Jazeera · Philippine Daily Inquirer · Rappler · Philippine News Agency · 2026-09-14

The Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) held its first parliamentary election on September 14. According to the Philippine Daily Inquirer, 2.393 million voters were registered across 1,186 voting centres and 5,212 clustered precincts, and each voter made eight choices — one regional political party, one district representative and six sectoral representatives. The parliament has 80 seats: 40 party representatives, 32 district representatives and eight sectoral representatives drawn from settler communities, women, youth, traditional leaders and the ulama. Priority voting for older people, persons with disabilities and pregnant women opened at 5am, with regular voting from 7am to 7pm (11:00 GMT); Al Jazeera reported that some precincts stayed open until 9pm because of late openings. Several violent incidents were recorded. Al Jazeera and the Inquirer reported a shooting near a school polling station in Cotabato City on the night of September 13 that killed three people and wounded 15, a second shooting in the city at about 7am on September 14 that wounded four including a police officer, and an improvised bomb that injured the person carrying it. The Inquirer reported that pre-shaded ballots were found in 36 of 68 precincts in Maluso, Basilan, requiring reprints. The region was created in 2019 under a 2014 peace deal, replacing the transition body led by the Moro Islamic Liberation Front; Al Jazeera puts the local conflict's death toll since the 1970s at about 150,000. 大西洋飓风季9月12日创卫星时代「最晚首个飓风」纪录，整季至今零飓风

CNN · ABC News · weather.com · Newsweek · 2026-09-12 / 2026-09-14

据CNN，截至9月12日大西洋尚未生成任何飓风，刷新了卫星时代「首个飓风出现最晚」的纪录；CNN称此时距本季开始已103天。据ABC新闻，此前的纪录日期为9月11日，由2002年的「古斯塔夫」（Gustav）与2013年的「温贝托」（Humberto）共同保持；ABC所引卫星纪录起始年份为1966年。本季大西洋迄今共生成5个获命名风暴——Arthur、Bertha、Cristobal、Dolly、Edouard——全部停留在热带风暴级别。ABC援引美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）数据称，常年到9月10日的平均值为8个命名风暴、3个飓风。weather.com称这是1950年以来最不活跃的开局。NOAA首席季节性飓风预报员马修·罗森克兰斯（Matthew Rosencrans）表示：「厄尔尼诺会加大热带大西洋的垂直风切变，信号在加勒比海上空最强。」迈阿密大学的安迪·黑泽尔顿（Andy Hazelton）对ABC说：「太平洋的活跃程度与2015年这类此前的极强厄尔尼诺事件相当」，并称「一旦出现极窄的窗口，仍可能有系统生成，各方应保持警惕」。本报只记录观测与预报机构的公开数据，不作气候归因判断。交叉核验：CNN · ABC News · weather.com · Newsweek

On September 12 the Atlantic Set a Satellite-Era Record for the Latest First Hurricane — and Still Has None

CNN · ABC News · weather.com · Newsweek · 2026-09-12 / 2026-09-14

According to CNN, no hurricane had formed in the Atlantic as of September 12, setting a satellite-era record for the latest first hurricane of a season; CNN put the figure at 103 days into the season. ABC News reported that the previous record date was September 11, shared by Gustav in 2002 and Humberto in 2013, and dated the satellite record it cites to 1966. The Atlantic has produced five named storms so far this season — Arthur, Bertha, Cristobal, Dolly and Edouard — all of which stayed at tropical-storm strength. Citing the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, ABC gave the long-term average by September 10 as eight named storms and three hurricanes. Weather.com called it the least active start since 1950. Matthew Rosencrans, NOAA's lead seasonal hurricane forecaster, said: "El Nino increases vertical wind shear over the tropical Atlantic, with the strongest signal over the Caribbean." Andy Hazelton of the University of Miami told ABC that "the activity in the Pacific is fairly similar to previous very strong El Nino events like 2015," and that "folks should stay vigilant in case a system does find a very narrow window." 塞拉莱那张没摊开的海图：一份临时通航方案，和它在9月13日被推迟的四天

综合信源：Al Jazeera · CNN · Bloomberg · Oman Observer（援引《金融时报》） · Middle East Monitor · Semafor · AP · S&P Global

方案本身

据半岛电视台9月14日的报道，原定在塞拉莱提交的是一份「临时」安排，不是重开霍尔木兹海峡。其要点为：为商船设立临时进出走廊，入境航道走伊朗水域，出境航道主要走阿曼水域；设立自愿性通行费，用于助航、环境保护与搜救；精确的海图与航路细节原定在会上分发。伊朗方面反复强调，这不等于海峡全面重开。据阿曼《观察家报》援引《金融时报》，德黑兰与马斯喀特此前已就该临时安排达成一致，其有效期至伊朗与海湾邻国就该水道的未来地位达成协议为止。

四天时间线

9月10日至11日：多架无人机袭击沙特阿拉伯东西输油管道，据Semafor与半岛电视台，该管道随后关闭；据CNBC，这条绕开霍尔木兹海峡的旁路涉及全球约4%的石油供应。9月11日：《金融时报》报道海湾国家考虑于下周与伊朗会面，彭博社同日报道称会议可能安排在塞拉莱。同期，胡塞武装控制也门穆哈港，据标普全球，穆哈及附近迈永岛的易手削弱了沙特红海出口通道的韧性。9月13日：巴林表态不出席；沙特阿拉伯提交修正案并要求推迟；阿曼外交大臣布赛迪当日在X上宣布会议推迟。9月14日：半岛电视台报道，会议未设新日期。

三方各自的表述

伊朗外交部发言人巴加埃称：「我们将与阿曼协商，在下一步决定如何宣布或登记该协议。」据半岛电视台，伊朗方面将推迟归因于沙特的反对，但未说明细节。据CNN与Axios，沙特的顾虑在于方案措辞可能「确立一种利雅得无法接受的新现状」。巴林的公开理由是，在伊朗自2月以来的袭击之后出席相当于「绥靖」。据半岛电视台援引的报道，巴林还提出，霍尔木兹海峡应当在不设歧视、不收费、不需许可的前提下保持开放。

背景：这是2月以来的第一次

据阿曼《观察家报》援引《金融时报》，这原本将是2026年2月美国与以色列对伊朗开战以来，海合会六国外长与伊朗高级官员的首次会晤；阿拉格齐上一次出席海合会层级的会议是2024年10月在多哈。伊朗自2026年2月28日起对正常商业通航关闭霍尔木兹海峡。据美联社9月14日的市场报道，布伦特原油当日报每桶107.37美元。

半岛电视台援引国际危机组织中东与北非项目副主任阿里·瓦埃兹（Ali Vaez）的分析称：「地区认可面越广，美国就越难把这一安排斥为伊朗的单方面强加。」这是被引述的第三方观点，不代表本报判断。可核验的是上述日期、当事方公开表态原文与已公布的价格数据；不可核验的是塞拉莱会议的完整议程、参会名单与新会期。本报不预测会谈是否重启，也不评价任何一方的政策。

Not a Single Hurricane: Reading the Atlantic's Blank Season Alongside a Record El Niño

Sources: CNN · ABC News · weather.com · Newsweek · Fox Weather · NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory · The Boston Globe

The record

According to CNN, as of September 12 the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season had produced no hurricane at all — a new satellite-era record for the latest first hurricane; CNN put it at 103 days into the season. ABC News reported that the previous record date was September 11, shared by Gustav in 2002 and Humberto in 2013, and dated the satellite record it cites to 1966. Five named storms have formed this season — Arthur, Bertha, Cristobal, Dolly and Edouard — none reaching hurricane strength. Weather.com called it the least active start since 1950. Citing NOAA, ABC gave the long-term average by September 10 as eight named storms and three hurricanes.

The mechanism: wind shear

According to CNN, wind shear across the Atlantic's main development region — running from the African coast to the Caribbean — reached record strength this summer; that region normally generates most storms from mid-August to early October. Matthew Rosencrans, NOAA's lead seasonal hurricane forecaster, said: "El Nino increases vertical wind shear over the tropical Atlantic, with the strongest signal over the Caribbean." That is the explanation given by the observing and forecasting agencies, recorded here without extension.

The numbers on the El Niño side

In a September 14 report by CNN meteorologist Mary Gilbert, this El Niño was declared in June 2026; as of September 14 the Relative Oceanic Niño Index for the Niño 3.4 region stood at 2.0C (3.6F) above average, crossing the "very strong" threshold and the highest on record for that calendar date. The same report gave NOAA's probability that the event will peak as the strongest since records began in 1950 at 75 per cent by the end of the year. RONI, adopted in February 2026, subtracts background warming from the sea-surface anomaly in order to measure El Niño's "true" strength. Fox Weather reported that NOAA's September outlook put the chance of a "super El Niño" this autumn and winter at 97 per cent. The September prediction from NOAA's Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory states that all 30 ensemble members show the event will "compete with, if not surpass, the strongest events in the historical record," while noting a slightly weaker predicted peak than the previous month's forecast.

The other side: the Pacific

According to ABC News, the eastern Pacific has produced 14 named storms and five hurricanes this year, against an average by this point of 10 named storms and five hurricanes. CNN's figures in early September were 13 named storms, four hurricanes and two major hurricanes. The two sets were compiled at different points and are given here as published. CNN's September 14 report also records 16 storms formed in the Pacific this year, two of them reaching Category 5, and three tropical systems affecting Hawaii within a month. Andy Hazelton of the University of Miami told ABC that "the activity in the Pacific is fairly similar to previous very strong El Nino events like 2015," adding that "folks should stay vigilant in case a system does find a very narrow window."

What is and is not verifiable

Verifiable: the storm names and counts, record dates, index values and probability ranges above, which are consistent across CNN, ABC News, weather.com, Fox Weather and NOAA's own publications. Not verifiable: whether a hurricane will form in the remainder of the season, and where this El Niño will ultimately peak. This publication makes no forecast and offers no climate attribution based on a single season's statistics.
