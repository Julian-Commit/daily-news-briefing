Indonesia's Anak Krakatau Erupts Repeatedly; Six Airports Halt Operations and 301 Flights Affected

Al Jazeera · The Washington Post · U.S. News · WTOP · 2026-09-06

Anak Krakatau, the volcanic island in the Sunda Strait between Java and Sumatra, began erupting late on September 5, with two further eruptions recorded on September 6. Indonesia's Geological Agency said the volcano had shown signs of activity since Friday before erupting for 30 seconds at 3:53am on Sunday. Ash columns rose to 6,000 metres (20,000 feet) on the Java side and up to 15,000 metres (50,000 feet) toward Sumatra and Banten province; the eruption was audible up to 700km away. Six Indonesian airports suspended operations. Jakarta's Soekarno-Hatta International, more than 150km from the volcano, halted operations until 5:30pm local time (10:30 GMT); airport operator Angkasa Pura said 301 flights were affected, including 58 international services. Husein Sastranegara airport in Bandung and Radin Inten II in Bandar Lampung also suspended flights, with Semarang and Surabaya designated as alternates. Singapore Airlines cancelled all flights to and from Jakarta for the rest of the day. Indonesia's disaster mitigation agency BNPB said there was no imminent tsunami risk, issued no evacuation order and reported no casualties, and announced cloud-seeding to encourage rain to wash away the ash. 停火框架之下仍在累积的数字：加沙的遗骸清理与黎巴嫩南部的持续空袭

综合信源：Al Jazeera（三篇）· 加沙卫生部 · 加沙民防部门 · 黎巴嫩官方统计 · 6月美伊谅解备忘录文本

宰敦区的一次集中下葬

9月6日，加沙城宰敦区（Zeitoun）的民防人员与遇难者家属从废墟中清出约100人的遗骸，随后在马马达尼（al-Ma'madani）墓地下葬。据加沙民防部门负责人拉伊德·达赫沙恩（Raed al-Dahshan）介绍，这批遗骸中包括60名儿童，且多为碎片而非完整遗体；仅马拉卡（Malaka）一个家族就在这一处遗址失去55名成员。达赫沙恩表示，废墟下仍约有8,000具遗体，若有足够设备，可在90天内完成挖掘；目前重型机械与防护装备均短缺，作业进度受限。

累计伤亡口径

加沙卫生部的统计为：自2023年10月以来至少73,651名巴勒斯坦人死亡、174,592人受伤。这一数字不含仍埋在废墟下、尚未确认身份的遗体。

黎巴嫩：9月6日凌晨的四处空袭

同一天凌晨，以色列对黎巴嫩南部纳巴提耶（Nabatieh）、阿拉伯萨利姆（Arab Salim）、上纳巴提耶（Nabatieh al-Fawqa）与卡法尔雷曼（Kfar Reman）实施空袭，造成至少4人死亡、20人受伤。其中阿拉伯萨利姆有两名女性死亡、另有六人受伤；上纳巴提耶两人死亡。纳巴提耶一座历史建筑被摧毁，赛义达·马苏梅花园（Sayyida Masouma Garden）市政公园被击中，卡法尔雷曼多处民宅被毁。以方称此次空袭是对真主党向以军发射两架无人机的回应，称后者构成"公然违反"。

停火框架的实际状态

今年6月，美伊签署谅解备忘录后地区袭击频率一度下降，以色列与黎巴嫩也在同月签署框架协议。但以方空袭持续，同时在其自行划定的、深入黎巴嫩境内约10公里的"安全区"内维持地面行动。黎巴嫩方面表示，这些袭击发生在停火安排仍然有效的情况下。

黎巴嫩一侧的累计口径

黎巴嫩官方统计显示，自2023年以来以色列的袭击已造成近9,000人死亡。加沙与黎巴嫩两处的共同点是：在被称为"停火"或"框架协议"的时期内，伤亡统计仍在逐日增加，而其中相当一部分来自尚未完成的废墟清理工作。

Counts That Keep Rising Under a Ceasefire Framework: Gaza's Rubble Recovery and Continued Strikes in South Lebanon

Sources: Al Jazeera (three reports) · Gaza Health Ministry · Gaza Civil Defence · Lebanese official tallies · June US-Iran memorandum of understanding

A mass burial in Zeitoun

On September 6, civil defence crews and relatives in Gaza City's Zeitoun neighbourhood recovered the remains of roughly 100 people from rubble and buried them at al-Ma'madani cemetery. Raed al-Dahshan, head of Gaza's civil defence, said the remains included 60 children and consisted largely of fragments rather than complete bodies; the Malaka family alone lost 55 members at this single site. Al-Dahshan said approximately 8,000 bodies remain under rubble and could be recovered within 90 days given adequate equipment; shortages of heavy machinery and protective gear are limiting the work.

Cumulative casualty figures

Gaza's Health Ministry reports at least 73,651 Palestinians killed and 174,592 wounded since October 2023. That figure does not include bodies still buried under rubble and not yet identified.

Lebanon: four strike locations before dawn on September 6

Before dawn the same day, Israel struck Nabatieh, Arab Salim, Nabatieh al-Fawqa and Kfar Reman in southern Lebanon, killing at least four people and wounding 20. Two women were killed in Arab Salim, where six others were wounded; two people were killed in Nabatieh al-Fawqa. A historical building in Nabatieh was destroyed, the Sayyida Masouma Garden municipal park was hit, and civilian homes were destroyed in Kfar Reman. Israel said the strikes responded to Hezbollah launching two drones toward Israeli forces, calling that a "blatant violation."

The state of the ceasefire framework

A US-Iran memorandum of understanding in June reduced the frequency of attacks across the region, and Israel and Lebanon signed a framework agreement the same month. Israeli strikes have nonetheless continued alongside ground operations inside a self-declared "security zone" roughly 10km into Lebanon. Lebanon says the attacks occurred while truce arrangements remained in force.

The cumulative count on the Lebanese side

Lebanese official tallies record nearly 9,000 deaths from Israeli attacks since 2023. What Gaza and Lebanon share is this: during a period described as a ceasefire or framework agreement, casualty counts continue to rise daily — and a substantial share of that increase comes from rubble clearance that is still unfinished.

On September 1, the G20 technology ministerial met in Chapel Hill, North Carolina. The EU: from entry into force to enforcement

The EU's AI Act prohibits AI activities posing "unacceptable risk" and imposes transparency standards. Its transparency rules took effect in August 2026, requiring certain AI systems to tell users when they are interacting with AI and when content has been generated or altered by it. 世界气象组织：本轮厄尔尼诺可能"超出四十年来使用的图表范围"

综合信源：Al Jazeera（三篇）· 世界气象组织（WMO）· 联合国粮农组织（FAO）· 世界粮食计划署（WFP）· 联合国秘书长办公室 · 联合国环境规划署（UNEP）

具体数值

5月至7月平均海表温度较常年偏高1.5°C。7月下旬至8月中旬的周值偏高2.2°C至2.6°C。7月至8月初，部分区域的次表层海水温度较常年偏高超过8°C。WMO预计厄尔尼诺将在2026年年末达到峰值，气候影响延续至2027年。

海洋背景：8月创下的记录

2026年上半年全球大部分海域持续呈现偏高的海表温度与大范围海洋热浪。8月下旬，全球平均海表温度升至21.1°C，为有记录以来最高，超过2024年3月创下的21.09°C。海水变暖通过热膨胀推高海平面，并提高海洋热浪的发生概率与强度，可能损害珊瑚礁与海草床、扰乱食物链。

已经出现的传导：食品价格

联合国粮农组织9月4日公布，8月食品价格指数达133.3点，为四年新高，且为连续第三个月上涨；谷物、植物油、糖、肉类与乳制品五个分类全部上涨，其中糖价较7月上涨近12%、达一年来最高，谷物价格上涨逾2%、为两年来最高。粮农组织首席经济学家马克西莫·托雷罗（Maximo Torero）表示"气候冲击、地缘政治紧张与物流中断正在汇合，收紧供应预期"。具体扰动包括黑海军事打击影响俄罗斯与乌克兰的小麦与玉米出口，以及霍尔木兹海峡封锁对全球化肥供应的挤压。欧洲夏季热浪造成作物单产下降，亚洲则面临厄尔尼诺威胁。世界粮食计划署警告，厄尔尼诺可能在明年年底前使额外5,000万人陷入严重饥饿。

与此同时，印尼加里曼丹与苏门答腊的林火产生的跨境烟霾促使马来西亚在砂拉越宣布紧急状态；8月，格陵兰彼得曼冰川崩解出一座面积逾76平方公里的冰山，为北极自2020年以来最大的冰川崩解事件。联合国环境规划署此前表示，世界将越过1.5°C升温线，但仍可限制、适应并从更高的温度回落。第31届联合国气候变化大会（COP31）定于11月9日至20日在土耳其安塔利亚会展中心举行，海洋议题已被多国列为诉求之一。

WMO: This El Niño May Be "Off the Charts We Have Used for the Past Four Decades"

Sources: Al Jazeera (three reports) · World Meteorological Organization · FAO · World Food Programme · UN Secretary-General's office · UNEP

The September 3 forecast update

The World Meteorological Organization issued its latest El Niño/La Niña update on September 3, expressing what it called the strongest certainty level it has ever stated in such an update: a nearly 100% likelihood that El Niño continues through February. WMO Secretary-General Celeste Saulo said "El Nino is firmly established and will intensify into a very strong event," and that the event "may be stronger than anything since our monitoring began — so literally off the charts we have used for the past four decades." UN Secretary-General António Guterres said "the planet is in uncharted waters, and those waters are heating up."

The figures

Sea surface temperatures averaged 1.5°C above normal from May to July. Weekly values from late July to mid-August ran 2.2°C to 2.6°C above average. From July to early August, subsurface ocean temperatures in some areas exceeded 8°C above average. WMO expects El Niño to peak in late 2026, with climate impacts extending into 2027.

Ocean context: a record set in August

The first half of 2026 was characterised by persistently elevated sea-surface temperatures and widespread marine heatwaves across much of the global ocean. In late August, the average global sea surface temperature reached 21.1°C, the highest on record, surpassing the previous record of 21.09°C set in March 2024. Warmer oceans raise sea levels through thermal expansion and increase the likelihood and intensity of marine heatwaves, which can damage coral reefs and seagrass beds and disrupt food chains.

A transmission already visible: food prices

The UN Food and Agriculture Organization reported on September 4 that its Food Price Index reached 133.3 points in August, a four-year high and a third consecutive monthly increase. All five tracked categories rose — cereals, vegetable oils, sugar, meat and dairy — with sugar up nearly 12% from July to its highest in a year and cereals up more than 2% to a two-year high. FAO chief economist Maximo Torero said "climate shocks, geopolitical tensions and disrupted trade logistics are converging to tighten supply expectations." Specific disruptions included Black Sea military strikes affecting wheat and maize shipments from Russia and Ukraine, and the Strait of Hormuz blockade straining global fertiliser supplies. European crop yields fell after summer heatwaves, while Asia faces El Niño risk. The World Food Programme warned that El Niño could push an additional 50 million people into acute hunger by the end of next year.

Events running in parallel

Meanwhile, transboundary haze from wildfires in Indonesia's Kalimantan and Sumatra prompted Malaysia to declare an emergency in Sarawak. In August, a giant iceberg measuring more than 76 square kilometres broke from Greenland's Petermann Glacier, the Arctic's largest glacier calving event since 2020.
