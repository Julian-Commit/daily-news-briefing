On September 30, the high-school blockade protests in France spread nationwide. French High-School Blockades Spread Nationwide: Interior Ministry Says 440 Detained on September 29, About 400 Schools Affected

Al Jazeera · Euronews · September 29–30, 2026

Al Jazeera reported on September 30 that French Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez said 440 people were detained nationwide on September 29 in operations linked to high-school blockades, with roughly two-thirds subsequently placed in police custody (garde à vue). Citing education ministry figures, Al Jazeera said about 400 of France’s 3,700 lycées were affected; 48 law-enforcement officers were injured that day, and seven incidents of hydrochloric acid being thrown at police were recorded. Euronews reported on September 29 that 164 people had already been arrested on September 28, with 180 schools affected. According to Euronews, the protests began at secondary schools in the Île-de-France region before spreading to Lille, Nantes, Marseille, Bordeaux, Lyon, Rennes and Seine-Saint-Denis. Students’ stated grievances include overcrowded classes (recorded by Al Jazeera as 32 to 35 pupils per class), teacher shortages, poor building conditions and long timetables; one placard recorded by Al Jazeera read: “8am-6pm. More hours of lessons than sleep.” Euronews quoted Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu as saying: “Fireworks, fires, violence near high schools: nothing justifies endangering students”; Nuñez said, “We cannot tolerate excesses.” Al Jazeera reported that the protests coincided with a public-sector strike over the government’s budget, with just over 11% of teaching staff taking part.

Trump and AI Company Leaders Sign a Voluntary “Accord on Superintelligence” at the White House: Internal Controls, External Audits and Board Review

NPR (via NPR Illinois) · Euronews · ABC News · September 29–30, 2026

NPR (via NPR Illinois) and Euronews reported on September 30 that U.S. President Donald Trump met artificial-intelligence and technology company leaders at the White House on September 29 and jointly signed a voluntary accord. ABC News reported the document’s full title as “The White House Accord on Superintelligence: A Joint Commitment on Frontier SI Responsibilities”, and said Trump described it at the event as a “constitution”. According to NPR and Euronews, the accord contains three elements: companies implementing “robust internal controls”; partnering with an “independent external auditor” to assess whether those controls are working; and establishing a committee within each company’s board of directors to review internal and external audit reports. The signatories and attendees recorded by NPR and Euronews include Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, Google CEO Sundar Pichai, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI President Greg Brockman, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Elon Musk; ABC News additionally recorded the attendance of Palantir’s Alex Karp and Shyam Sankar, Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, and House Speaker Mike Johnson. NPR quoted Trump as saying: “I think I’m seeing tremendous self-policing. And they understand that they have to self-police.” Asked whether the agreement is binding, he said it is “morally” binding. ABC News quoted Johnson as saying the document is “a statement of principles…a statement of standards, commitments that are voluntary on behalf of the industry”. NPR quoted Robin Jia, a researcher at the University of Southern California, as sceptical of relying on self-policing, saying that “as the models get better, the consequences of such things are only going to get greater”.

Remnants of Hurricane Polo Flood the U.S. Southwest: New Mexico’s McLeod Dam at Risk of Failure, Five Communities Evacuated in Turn

NPR (via WBGO) · KRWG Public Media · KVIA · September 29–30, 2026

NPR, carried by WBGO on September 30, reported that the remnants of Hurricane Polo brought heavy rain to the U.S. Southwest, flooding roadways and forcing residents of several small New Mexico towns to evacuate over concerns that a dam could fail. KRWG Public Media and KVIA reported that on the night of September 29, Doña Ana County issued evacuation orders in turn for Garfield, Salem, Rincon, Hatch and Placitas. NPR reported that Polo struck Baja California Sur with maximum sustained winds of 110 mph (177 kph), crossed the Gulf of California and weakened to a tropical storm as it moved inland to the northeast. According to NPR, a person was rescued from an overturned vehicle west of Phoenix, Arizona, and a truck was swept away north of Phoenix; the Bureau of Land Management reported significant flooding and road damage in conservation areas in the state’s southeast. NPR quoted Carter Greulich, a meteorologist with the weather service in Albuquerque: “Don’t drive through flooded roadways. Remain out of the arroyos, especially in our urban areas.” No deaths were recorded in the report.

A Drone Crashes and Explodes Inside Moldova During a Large Russian Strike on Ukraine; Romania Scrambles Two F-18s

The Kyiv Independent · LIGA.net · Mezha · September 30, 2026

The Kyiv Independent and LIGA.net reported on September 30 that a drone entered Moldovan airspace at about 5:43 a.m. local time that morning and then fell and exploded near a village in the Anenii Noi district; the village is rendered as Hirbovat by the Kyiv Independent and as Girbovets by LIGA.net. Citing the Moldovan account, LIGA.net reported that preliminary findings indicate the point of impact and explosion was at a height of about 40 metres above ground level, near a communications antenna, with drone fragments found within a radius of about 265 metres and communication services unaffected. Both outlets reported that, according to preliminary information, no injuries occurred. The Kyiv Independent placed the site about 20 kilometres from the border with Ukraine’s Odesa region. Citing Ukraine’s air force, the Kyiv Independent reported that Russia attacked Ukraine overnight with 188 drones (86 of them jet-powered) along with Zircon, Onyx and Iskander missiles, and that Ukrainian forces “shot down or suppressed 5 missiles and 155 drones”. Both outlets reported that Romania’s Defence Ministry confirmed two F-18 fighter jets were scrambled after radars detected an aerial target; LIGA.net identified them as F-18s of the Spanish Air and Space Force. The jets tracked the target near the Ukrainian border east of Izmail, and no object was detected inside Romanian airspace.

深度求索开源与华为联合开发的昇腾芯片编程工具，核心组件 TileLang 被定位为英伟达 CUDA 的替代方案

南华早报（SCMP） · 彭博社（经《自由今日大马》转载） · Techzine · DQIndia · 2026-09-30

据南华早报9月30日报道，中国人工智能企业深度求索（DeepSeek）公开发布了与华为技术有限公司合作开发的软件工具，用于为华为昇腾（Ascend）系列人工智能芯片编程，并以开源方式免费提供。据南华早报与 Techzine，本次发布的组件共六个：TileLang、DeepGEMM、DeepEP、TileKernels、FlashMLA 与 DeepSelect。据 Techzine 与 DQIndia，TileLang 是一种面向人工智能芯片的开源编程语言，允许开发者在较高抽象层级上描述需要执行的计算，再由软件转译到底层硬件；DeepGEMM 负责矩阵运算，DeepEP 处理大模型负载下的分布式通信，TileKernels 覆盖向量计算与内存访问，FlashMLA 支持深度求索的注意力架构，DeepSelect 负责数据筛选。据彭博社（经《自由今日大马》9月30日转载），TileLang 被定位为英伟达 CUDA 的中国替代方案；报道并称昇腾 950 获得了针对性的性能优化，深度求索计划在内蒙古一处数据中心部署至少16万颗华为最强算力加速器。据 Techzine，双方还合作开发了一个由128颗昇腾 950 处理器组成的超节点，对计算分配与芯片间通信均作了优化。

DeepSeek Open-Sources Ascend Programming Tools Built With Huawei; Core Component TileLang Is Positioned as an Alternative to Nvidia’s CUDA

South China Morning Post · Bloomberg (via Free Malaysia Today) · Techzine · DQIndia · September 30, 2026

The South China Morning Post reported on September 30 that Chinese artificial-intelligence company DeepSeek publicly released software tools, developed with Huawei Technologies, for programming Huawei’s Ascend AI chips, making them open-source and free to download. According to the SCMP and Techzine, six components were released: TileLang, DeepGEMM, DeepEP, TileKernels, FlashMLA and DeepSelect. Techzine and DQIndia describe TileLang as an open-source programming language for AI chips that lets developers specify computations at a higher level of abstraction while the software translates them to the underlying hardware; DeepGEMM handles matrix operations, DeepEP addresses distributed communication for large-model workloads, TileKernels covers vector computation and memory access, FlashMLA supports DeepSeek’s attention architecture, and DeepSelect handles data filtering. Bloomberg, carried by Free Malaysia Today on September 30, reported that TileLang is positioned as China’s answer to Nvidia’s CUDA, that the Ascend 950 received specific performance optimisation, and that DeepSeek plans to deploy at least 160,000 of Huawei’s most powerful accelerators at a data-centre facility in Inner Mongolia. Techzine reported that the two companies also worked on a supernode of 128 Ascend 950 processors, with both the distribution of computation and inter-chip communication optimised.
