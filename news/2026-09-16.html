All four pleaded not guilty and all may appeal. On reaction, Euronews reported that Kosovo's Foreign Minister Glauk Konjufca called the ruling "a sad, unjust, undeserved decision"; thousands had gathered in Prishtina's main square to await the verdict, and the square emptied within minutes of the guilty findings being read. The EU Moves to Ban Social Media for Under-13s, with a Kids Act That Would Put the Burden of Proving Safety on Platforms

综合信源：Al Jazeera · Euronews · CNN · AP（经多家地方台） · The National · 科索沃特别法庭公开信息

判决的两半
