美伊冲突九日激战：从停火协议到霍尔木兹海峡全面对抗

综合信源：Al Jazeera · BBC · ABC News · Euronews · Gulf News · Reuters

冲突的起点与升级

美伊本轮冲突始于2026年2月28日，美国与以色列对伊朗发动联合打击。经过数月激战，双方于6月中旬达成一项临时停火协议，设定60天期限以谈判永久和平方案——包括伊朗核计划等核心议题。然而，该协议在7月8日因特朗普宣布其"已结束"而破裂。

霍尔木兹海峡争夺

全球约五分之一石油供应经由霍尔木兹海峡通行。战前每日约140艘船只穿行该水道。自冲突爆发以来，海峡航运量大幅萎缩——7月18日至19日分别仅有8艘和3艘过境。美方上一周重新对伊朗港口实施海上封锁，称已强行改道6艘商船并瘫痪1艘。伊朗方面则以攻击"不安全通道"中的船只作为回应，警告"只要美军行动继续，该水道不会安全运输一滴石油和天然气"。

人员伤亡与基础设施破坏

自2月28日开战以来，美军阵亡人数已升至17人。最近三名阵亡者中，两人（一等兵Isabella Gonzales，19岁；中尉Tyler James Feehan，25岁）于7月18日在约旦的伊朗导弹和无人机袭击中丧生，第三人在7月19日于伊拉克北部的伊朗无人机"控制引爆"中死亡。伊朗方面称近期美国空袭已造成至少50人死亡、517人受伤。美国上周末的打击还摧毁了伊朗南部霍尔木兹甘省一座海水淡化厂，切断了约1万人的供水，并损坏了位于格什姆岛的另一座淡化设施。

地区扩散

冲突正从美伊两国之间向整个中东地区外溢。伊朗在报复打击中将目标对准美国在海湾的盟友：科威特一座发电与海水淡化厂两日内第二次遭袭；约旦军方称拦截数枚伊朗导弹；巴林则多次拉响空袭警报。也门胡塞武装于7月20日宣布对沙特实施海上封锁，作为对萨那国际机场遭袭的回应——这标志着该组织数年来首次以上述规模直接对沙特采取军事行动。海湾合作委员会（GCC）秘书长指控伊朗的袭击构成"战争罪"。

经济冲击

布伦特原油周一突破每桶90美元，为逾一个月来首次。美国汽油均价回升至每加仑4美元。韩国已敦促本国公民撤离中东。全球多地加油站排长队、油价飙升，通胀压力再度上升。

外交层面的僵局

伊朗最高领袖莫杰塔巴·哈梅内伊称特朗普在停火协议上的签名为"无效且毫无价值"，伊朗谈判代表宣布暂停履行协议承诺。伊朗总统佩泽什基安承认国家正处于"全面战争"之中。美国国务卿鲁比奥则在前往菲律宾参加东盟外长会议前表示，美国仍对"真正的外交解决方案"持开放态度，但"伊朗必须愿意遵守协议"。

Nine Days of US-Iran Escalation: From Ceasefire Collapse to All-Out Hormuz Standoff

Sources: Al Jazeera · BBC · ABC News · Euronews · Gulf News · Reuters

Origins and Escalation

The current US-Iran conflict began on February 28, 2026, with joint US-Israeli strikes on Iran. After months of fighting, an interim ceasefire was reached in mid-June, setting a 60-day window to negotiate a permanent peace — including Iran's nuclear program. The deal collapsed on July 8 when Trump declared it "over."

The Strait of Hormuz Standoff

Roughly one-fifth of global oil supplies transited the Strait of Hormuz before the war — about 140 vessels daily. Since the conflict, traffic has dwindled to 3–8 vessels per day. The US reimposed a naval blockade on Iranian ports last week, claiming to have redirected six ships and disabled one. Iran's IRGC declared the strait "unsafe for the transit of petrochemical products, nor even a single drop of oil and gas" while US military operations persist.

Casualties and Infrastructure Damage

Seventeen US service members have been killed since February 28. The most recent: PFC Isabella Gonzales, 19, and 1st Lt. Tyler James Feehan, 25, killed in an Iranian attack on Jordan on July 18, and a third service member killed in a drone incident in Iraq on July 19. Iran reports at least 50 killed and 517 wounded in recent US strikes. US airstrikes also destroyed a desalination plant in Hormozgan province, cutting water to ~10,000 people.

Regional Spillover

Iran has retaliated by striking US allies across the Gulf: Kuwait's power and desalination plant attacked twice in two days; Jordan intercepted multiple missiles; Bahrain sounded repeated air-raid sirens. Yemen's Houthis announced a maritime blockade of Saudi Arabia on July 20 in response to the strike on Sanaa airport — its first such direct action against the Kingdom in years. The GCC Secretary-General accused Iran of "war crimes" for targeting civilian infrastructure.

Economic Impact

Brent crude topped $90/barrel on Monday for the first time in over a month. US gas prices hit an average of $4/gallon. South Korea urged its citizens to leave the Middle East. Global inflation concerns are mounting.

Diplomatic Impasse

Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei called Trump's signature on the interim deal "worthless and invalid." President Pezeshkian acknowledged Iran is in a "full-scale war." Secretary of State Rubio, en route to ASEAN meetings in the Philippines, said the US remains open to a "real diplomatic solution" but Iran must be "willing to live by it."

AI安全攻防战进入"机器速度"时代：从Hugging Face入侵到中美模型角力

综合信源：The Next Web · Help Net Security · Quartz · Daily Texas News · unrot.co · Axios

自主AI攻击成为现实

2026年7月16日，全球最大开源AI模型平台Hugging Face披露了一起前所未有的安全事件：一个完全自主的AI智能体入侵了其生产基础设施。攻击通过在数据管道中投递恶意数据集、利用两个代码执行路径获取初始访问权限，随后横向移动渗透多个内部集群，留下超过17,000个记录事件。其指挥控制（C2）服务器在公共托管服务间自动迁移以隐藏行踪。Hugging Face称攻击框架似乎是基于一个智能体安全研究工具构建的，但无法确定驱动攻击的是哪个底层模型，也无法判断该模型是经过越狱的商业模型还是完全不受限制的开源模型。

AI对AI的防御

Hugging Face的异常检测管道使用大语言模型（LLM）对安全数据进行分类，区分真实威胁和日常噪音。正是这套系统标记了入侵。随后，其安全团队部署了专门的AI分析智能体来处理数万个自动操作记录，在数小时内重建了完整攻击时间线、提取了入侵指标，并区分了真实损害和攻击者设置的分心诱饵。这一速度匹配了攻击方的"机器速度"——在传统人工调查方式下，同样的工作需要数天才能完成。

安全护栏的悖论

一个出人意料的转折是：当Hugging Face团队尝试使用商业API上的前沿模型进行取证分析时——这意味着"喂"给模型真实的攻击指令、利用代码和其他恶意产物——模型的安全护栏无法区分事件响应者和攻击者，拒绝了所有相关请求。团队最终转向使用中国智谱AI（Z.ai）的开源模型GLM 5.2，在自己的硬件上运行，成功完成了分析。Hugging Face由此得出一个直白的教训："攻击者不受任何使用政策的约束"，安全团队必须提前准备好可以在自有基础设施上运行的高能力模型。

AI供应链安全的新挑战

与此同时，路易斯维尔大学和北德克萨斯大学的研究人员发布了CodeTracer——一个专门追踪AI代码助手中由投毒训练数据引入的后门漏洞的取证工具。该工具在超过100万个Python源文件的测试集上保持低于3%的漏报率，每次仅需约47秒即可完成追踪，成本约为0.33美元。这一研究与Hugging Face事件共同指向同一个方向：AI供应链已成为前线目标，攻防双方均在加速部署AI能力。

中美AI竞争加速

在Hugging Face选择中国模型进行取证的同一周，北京月之暗面公司发布了Kimi K3——全球首个开源2.8万亿参数模型，在多个基准测试上逼近甚至超越美国前沿模型。特朗普政府同期推出"金鹰"（Gold Eagle）计划，将前沿AI模型的访问审批权集中到白宫——此举标志着美国AI政策从6月的自愿申报框架迅速走向政府强力干预。美国商务部上月还曾以国家安全为由强制Anthropic从全球撤回Fable 5和Mythos 5模型。前AI沙皇戴维·萨克斯（David Sacks）评论Kimi K3的表现"令人担忧"，称"这就是输掉AI竞赛的方式"。

AI Security Enters the "Machine Speed" Era: From the Hugging Face Breach to US-China AI Competition

Sources: The Next Web · Help Net Security · Quartz · Daily Texas News · unrot.co · Axios

Autonomous AI Attacks Become Reality

On July 16, 2026, Hugging Face disclosed an unprecedented breach: a fully autonomous AI agent penetrated its production infrastructure. The attack began with a malicious dataset exploiting two code-execution paths, then moved laterally across multiple internal clusters, leaving over 17,000 recorded events. Its C2 servers migrated autonomously across public hosting services to evade detection. Hugging Face said the framework appeared agentic security-research-based, but could not identify the underlying model.

AI-vs-AI Defense

Hugging Face's anomaly-detection pipeline uses an LLM to triage security data — it flagged the intrusion. The team then deployed dedicated AI analysis agents against tens of thousands of automated actions, reconstructing the attack timeline, extracting indicators of compromise, and separating real damage from decoy activity in hours — work that would have taken humans days.

The Guardrail Paradox

When Hugging Face tried to use commercial frontier models for forensic analysis, their safety guardrails blocked requests containing real attack commands and exploit code — unable to distinguish an incident responder from an attacker. The team switched to China's Z.ai GLM 5.2 open-weight model, running it on their own hardware. Hugging Face's lesson: "The attacker was bound by no usage policy, while our own forensic work was blocked by the guardrails of the hosted models we first tried."

AI Supply Chain Security

Separately, researchers from the University of Louisville and UNT released CodeTracer, a forensic tool that traces backdoored code completions from AI assistants to poisoned training data. Tested on over one million Python files, it achieved a false negative rate below 3% and ran at ~47 seconds per case at a cost of ~$0.33. Together with the Hugging Face incident, these developments signal that the AI supply chain is now a frontline target.

US-China AI Race Intensifies

In the same week Hugging Face turned to a Chinese model for forensics, Beijing-based Moonshot AI released Kimi K3 — the world's first open 2.8-trillion-parameter model — which matched or beat US frontier models on several benchmarks. The Trump administration simultaneously launched the "Gold Eagle" program, centralizing frontier AI access approval at the White House, a sharp escalation from the voluntary framework established in June. Former AI czar David Sacks called K3's performance "concerning," writing: "This is how you lose the AI race."
